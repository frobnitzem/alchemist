# Future Development Roadmap for Alchemist

## 1. Feature Development
### A. Higher-Order Geometric Descriptors
- Bond Angle Distributions: Capture the arrangement of neighbors, not just their identity.
- Local Density/Coordination: Describe local packing fractions and deviations from ideal lattice coordination.
- Multi-Species Support: Generalize composition features for ternary or quaternary alloys.

### B. Model Architecture Evolutions
- Attention-Based Aggregation: Replace simple sums with Graph Attention Network (GAT) layers to learn neighbor importance.
- Equivariant Layers: Implement E(3)-equivariant architectures to ensure rotation invariance.
- Adaptive Step-Sizing: Implement learned dt or sophisticated ODE solvers for the flow.

### C. Experimentation Framework
- Active Learning Loop: Identify high-variance regions in energy predictions to target for high-fidelity (DFT) data acquisition.

## 2. Scientific Hypotheses to Test
### Hypothesis 1: Local Environment Sufficiency
- Question: Is the 17-atom neighborhood (self + 4 NN1 + 12 NN2) sufficient for the energy landscape?
- Test: Compare energy prediction variance using only NN1 vs. NN1+NN2.

### Hypothesis 2: Compositional Correlation
- Question: Does the model capture clustering tendencies (e.g., Ga-As preference)?
- Test: Compare generated sum_neighbor_features statistics against known stable crystal structures.

### Hypothesis 3: Flow Depth vs. Sample Quality
- Question: Does increasing MultiStep iterations linearly improve distribution matching?
- Test: Evaluate statistics across a range of n_steps_flow (5, 10, 20, 50).

### Hypothesis 4: Sensitivity to Box Constraints
- Question: How does the model handle strain?
- Test: Vary the box size (compression/expansion) and observe shifts in generated neighbor features.
